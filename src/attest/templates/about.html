The claim boundary

Attest is a reviewable record of observations, not an authority on what physically happened. Every artifact states its own limits.

What a signed record DOES establish

  • These exact bytes were issued by this deployment's key, in this order, hash-chained.
  • Every review/correction is anchored to the original and append-only — nothing is silently rewritten.
  • The mutation journal makes post-hoc edits to stored rows detectable.
  • Poll coverage attests how much of a window the pipeline actually watched.
  • A worker's acknowledgment binds to the record's hash — agreement or dispute is on record.

What it does NOT establish

  • Identity. No face match, no ID check. "Self-reported" means exactly that.
  • Attendance or time worked. An observed interval is not a shift.
  • Absence. A silent window means no events were received — never proof nobody came.
  • Departure. Nobody certifies when anyone left, or that they stayed inside.
  • The model's word. Summaries are labeled by source and never treated as evidence.

Integrity surfaces

Signed receiptsEd25519 over canonical payload; hash-chained; replay-clock and wall-clock provenance in every payload.
Review chainEach revision signs over the previous hash and anchors to the original receipt.
Mutation journalEvery store write is a chained journal entry; attest journal or /api/journal replays it. Silent edits and row deletions surface as mismatches.
Dispute packpack.zip ships the bundle, media, and a zero-dependency verifier — check it on any Python install, no trust in this service.
Case pack/sites/{id}/pack.zip exports every signed record for a site with a manifest of receipt hashes and worker stances — for disputes about a pattern, not one visit.
Honest silencehistory_poll_coverage states how much of the window was watched; blind gaps are shown, not hidden.
