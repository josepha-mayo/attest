What this brief is — and is not. This page is a rendering of a signed Attest record. The signature proves the underlying record has not been altered since it was signed — it does not prove identity, attendance, time worked, or physical presence. Observed activity is a device report, not certification of presence; a quiet window is not proof nobody came{% if coverage and coverage.interruptions %}; where the channel itself reported an interruption, that cause is shown, but it still does not establish what happened physically{% endif %}. Worker and household statements are self-reported accounts kept verbatim; a coordinator conclusion is a human judgment appended to the record.

To verify independently: ask the coordinator for the signed export pack (pack.zip) for this record. Open its index.html in any browser — it re-verifies every signature offline — or use the hosted verifier or attest verify bundle.json --key <issuer key above>. The hashes above must match the signed payload exactly; any alteration invalidates the signature.
