Attest pack verifier

Verify an exported Attest case or dispute pack — entirely in your browser.

Drop a pack.zip exported from Attest, or a single signed receipt such as a verify-live --sign report. Every signature, payload hash, chain link, and manifest entry is checked locally — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

No pack handy? Open the verifier and press "Verify the bundled sample pack" — one click fetches a small simulated pack (media digests only) and runs every check in-page. Or download it and drop it on the verifier yourself. Its story rotated the signing key mid-week, then revoked the retired key as compromised — look for the key_rotation, key_adoption, and key_revocation attestations: receipts on both sides of the pivot still verify, the verifier annotates the suspect window, and it even flags that the pivot itself was signed by the compromised key — the trust handoff inherits the doubt. The pack also carries verifier_manifest.json — an issuer-signed receipt pinning the sha256 of its own verifier tooling, so a forged always-green script can't ride inside a genuine pack.

Or check our own claim: this signed receipt is the verify-live --sign sweep run against the real api.amazonvision.com on 2026-09-30 — 5 pass, 3 fail (Playground scope limits, honestly recorded). The signature proves the report is exactly what the API returned that day; it can't be edited to turn a fail into a pass.

Verifying a pack pins it to an issuer, not just a fingerprint: every deployment serves /.well-known/attest-issuer.json — the JWKS analogue — carrying the current issuer key plus every signed rotation/adoption/revocation receipt. attest verify pack.zip --issuer-url <deployment> (or a doc file exported by attest issuer --out) pins the pack to that deployment and walks the signed lifecycle itself, so a pack exported before a key rotation still verifies against the deployment's current key. Remote loads are HTTPS-only (loopback excepted) and byte-capped — and the document itself says the pin's authenticity rides on transport, so --key remains the zero-trust path.

What verification proves: the records in the pack are the records the issuing deployment signed, and nothing was added, removed, or altered since. It does not prove physical attendance, identity, or time worked — that boundary is the point of the project.
