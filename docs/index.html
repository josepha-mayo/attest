Attest pack verifier

Verify an exported Attest case or dispute pack — entirely in your browser.

Open the verifier →

Drop a pack.zip exported from Attest. Every signature, payload hash, chain link, and manifest entry is checked locally — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What verification proves: the records in the pack are the records the issuing deployment signed, and nothing was added, removed, or altered since. It does not prove physical attendance, identity, or time worked — that boundary is the point of the project.
