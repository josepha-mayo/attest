What this does and does not establish

A green result proves the signed records are intact and were issued under the pinned issuer key — integrity, not physical truth. It does not prove anyone was present, absent, or honest; it proves the evidence chain was not altered after signing. Media reported as "withheld" was deliberately redacted, not lost.

Trust note: this verifier file traveled inside the pack it is checking. For a dispute that matters, re-verify with a verifier obtained independently — the deployment's /verify-pack page, verify_case.py pinned from the issuing deployment, or the operator's hosted verifier — never trust only the copy a pack carries about itself.
